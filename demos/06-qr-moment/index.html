About This Festival

Cheltenham Paint Festival has been transforming the town's walls with large-scale public art since 2017. Each year, local and international artists are invited to create site-specific works across the town centre.

Many of the artworks remain long after the festival ends, creating a permanent public art trail. Use the map to find which pieces are still on display and navigate to them.
