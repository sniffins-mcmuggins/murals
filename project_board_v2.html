The Idea

A home built specifically for the world of paint festivals. Not a generic events tool. Not a portfolio site. Something that understands how festivals, public art, and working artists actually operate — and is designed entirely around helping artists get work.

What makes it different

A branded QR code on every wall at every festival drives visitors directly to artist profiles. The festival map stays live after the event ends — making public art discoverable permanently. Artist profiles compound over time. The platform gets richer with every festival, every year.

The weekly curated artist blog and annual print magazine give the platform a voice and a reason to open the app between festivals. No equivalent product exists anywhere in the world.

01

A professional profile, branded QR code for their wall, location-pinned portfolio collections, festival application management, and analytics that show exactly how many people scanned their wall. Almost free to join. Designed entirely to help them get work.
